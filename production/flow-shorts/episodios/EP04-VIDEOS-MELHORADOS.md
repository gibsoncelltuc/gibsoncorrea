```
CONTEXT: I am making a 60-second vertical kids' short as 6 video clips of 10 seconds each. Generate the 6 videos ONE AT A TIME, in numbered order (video 1, then 2, ... then 6). For each video, use the attached image with the same number as its FIRST FRAME (image 1 -> video 1, image 2 -> video 2, and so on). Use the attached character sheets SHEET-LUNA and SHEET-BENTO as the references for the characters. Each video is exactly 10 seconds, VERTICAL portrait 9:16 (no landscape, no blurred bars), 720p.

CONTINUITY: Keep the kitchen, the lighting, the objects and the characters exactly as in the first-frame image. Do not add or remove objects. The characters look identical to their sheets from the first frame to the last frame.

BENTO: a lop-eared bunny with exactly TWO soft floppy ears that ALWAYS hang down along both sides of his head, one on each side. His ears NEVER stand upright and he NEVER has a second pair of ears. Bento has exactly 2 eyes, 2 arms, 2 legs and 1 small round tail.
LUNA: a blue-gray kitten with exactly 2 eyes, 2 small pointed ears, 2 arms, 2 legs and 1 long tail.

STYLE: high-quality stylized 3D animated feature-film look, soft rounded shapes, big expressive eyes, fluffy fur, warm cinematic lighting, smooth natural character animation, no text, no letters, no logos, nothing scary. Keep the lower-left corner of the frame simple and uncluttered.

MOVEMENT RULES: one slow, simple, continuous action per clip, exactly as described; all body parts stay attached and in view; ears only sway gently; no fast spinning; no camera shake; no cuts inside the clip.

AUDIO: ONLY soft sound effects. No music, no singing, no speech.

NEGATIVE: upright or standing ears, a second pair of ears, three or four ears, extra or duplicated arms, legs, paws, toes, tails, eyes, heads; merged or melting limbs; a character splitting or morphing; color, size or outfit changes; characters appearing or disappearing; objects appearing or multiplying; text, letters, logos; scary or sudden elements.

VIDEO 1 (first frame = image 1; use SHEET-LUNA)

Action: 0-3 s Luna bounces gently on the stool and rubs her soapy paws together. 3-7 s she lifts both paws toward the camera to show the bubbles while a few bubbles float up. 7-10 s she smiles and gives her paws a small happy shake.
Camera: slow, steady push-in.
Last frame: Luna facing the camera with both soapy paws raised.
Sound effects: soft soap bubble pops, a light sparkle chime.

VIDEO 2 (first frame = image 2; use SHEET-BENTO and SHEET-LUNA)

Last frame: Bento looking toward the sink, Luna smiling.
Sound effects: a soft boing, playful xylophone notes, a small toy tap.

Action: 0-3 s Luna rubs her two paws together under the running water. 3-7 s she turns them over and rubs again while the foam grows. 7-10 s she looks up at the camera with a happy face, still rubbing.
Camera: steady close-up with a very slight push-in.
Last frame: Luna smiling at the camera over a sink full of foam.
Sound effects: running water, soft rubbing, bubble pops.

VIDEO 4 (first frame = image 4; use SHEET-BENTO and SHEET-LUNA)
First frame: Bento stands on a wooden stool at the left and Luna stands behind the sink at the right; both rub their bubbly paws together and smile. The faucet is running, soap bubbles float in the air, a table with apples is at the left, a pink soap dispenser and a yellow towel are on the right.
```
