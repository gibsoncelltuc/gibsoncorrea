Elon Musk promised a car that flies. And, in front of guests, his car lifted off the ground. It was October twenty twenty-six, at a SpaceX base, in Texas. The secret is SpaceX thrusters hidden in the car: ten nozzles that blow compressed air downward. And a suction system sticks the car to the track, like a suction cup, even upside down. In front of the guests, the car sped up, climbed a giant curved ramp and hung upside down. Then it let go, flew spinning through the air and landed in a cloud of white smoke. Then it took off and flew, on its thrusters, over parked vehicles. According to Tesla, it goes from zero to almost one hundred kilometers per hour in under one second. But experts pointed out: it lasted only a few seconds, it was not a real flight. Flying car or jumping car? You decide. Now don't forget to comment: what should the next video be about?
