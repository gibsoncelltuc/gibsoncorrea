A car lifted off the ground and flew in front of guests. And it was not a special effect.
It was Elon Musk's new invention, at a SpaceX base, in Texas, in October twenty twenty-six.
The first trick: a suction system sticks the car to the track, like a suction cup.
With it, the car sped up, climbed a giant curved ramp and hung upside down.
Then it let go, flew spinning through the air and landed in a cloud of white smoke.
The second trick: ten hidden nozzles, that blow compressed air downward.
That is how it took off and flew, on its thrusters, over parked vehicles.
And, according to Tesla, it goes from zero to almost one hundred kilometers per hour in under one second.
But experts pointed out: the flight lasted only a few seconds. It is not a real flying car yet.
