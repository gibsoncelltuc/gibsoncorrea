In thirteen twenty-five, two Italian cities went to war over a bucket.
It started when some soldiers from Modena sneaked into Bologna, and stole a wooden bucket from the town well.
Bologna was furious, and demanded it back. Modena refused. So they gathered thousands of knights, and marched into battle.
The armies met at Zappolino. Bologna had many more men, but Modena's cavalry charged, and the lines broke.
Modena won, and chased the enemy all the way to the gates of Bologna.
The Modenese rode home in triumph, carrying the bucket, and put it in their tower, as a trophy.
Seven hundred years later, it's still there. Bologna only has a copy.
Thousands died in the battle. And what did they fight over? A bucket.
